FAQs


How Do I Get There?

You can take a ferry from Tsawwassen that brings you straight to Galiano; it takes about 40 minutes once the ferry is moving. Then you can drive up to Bodega Ridge which is another 30 minutes. Try pressing on the people above to see who you can carpool with.


What is the Colour Scheme?

Sage green and lavender.


What Should I Wear?

Dress to impress! Suits and long dresses is what we're thinking, but keep in mind the weather might be warm.


What Am I Paying For?

You're on the hook for breakfast, lunches, and travel.


Can I bring a plus one?

Due to the size of the wedding we can only accommodate those who have received an invitation.


Do I need to book a ferry?

Yes! Please organize carpooling and book a ferry.


Where's the Wedding Registry?

We chose to opt-out of having a registry because we're already moved out into a little apartment. If you feel inclined to give a gift, it is more than welcome but not necessary :)
